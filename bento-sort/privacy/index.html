Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 29, 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected, used, and secured by Bento Sort ("the Game") when played on mobile iOS or Android devices.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

Bento Sort is a free-to-play puzzle game supported by third-party advertising and standard optional in-app purchases. To facilitate these services, we utilize standard industry software development kits (SDKs) which may collect certain device-level information:

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA or Android's Advertising ID) used to deliver personalized ads.
  • Technical Data: Your device model, operating system version, system language, and IP address (used to infer approximate regional location for appropriate currency displays).
  • Game Data: Local high scores, total coins collected, purchased bento skins, and ad-free unlock states are stored safely and persistently on your own device using local storage caching.

2. Third-Party Services We Integrate

The Game integrates secure third-party services that have their own independent privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial ads. AdMob utilizes anonymous identifiers to serve, personalize, and measure advertising performance. You can review Google's policy here: Google Privacy Policy.
  • App Store Billing In-app purchases (such as "Remove Ads" or purchasing coin bags) are handled securely through Apple's App Store Kit and Google Play Billing. We never collect, process, or store your credit card details or payment credentials.

3. How We Use Collected Data

Any automatically gathered device data is used exclusively to:

  • Serve appropriate, relevant banner and interstitial ads.
  • Detect regional locales to automatically display prices in local currencies (e.g. £, $, €, ¥).
  • Analyze game metrics to optimize game balance, difficulty, and visual theme displays.

4. Your Rights & Choice (Opt-Out)

You can easily restrict mobile advertising tracking or completely opt-out of personalized ads via your native device settings:

  • On iOS Devices: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to manage app permissions, or disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
  • On Android Devices: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads and click "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or reset your Advertiser ID.

5. Children's Privacy

Bento Sort is a wholesome, family-friendly sorting puzzle and does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children. If you believe any advertising identifiers are being processed incorrectly, please contact us immediately.
