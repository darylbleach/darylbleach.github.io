Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected, used, and secured by Little Allotment ("the Game") when played on mobile iOS or Android devices.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

Little Allotment is a free-to-play puzzle game supported by third-party advertising and one optional in-app purchase. To facilitate these services, we use standard industry software development kits (SDKs) which may collect certain device-level information:

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA) used to deliver ads.
  • Technical Data: Your device model, operating system version, and general region.
  • Garden Data: Your allotment's growth, streak, daily results, and ad-free purchase state are stored locally on your own device using local storage - never on a server we control.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google AdMob We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial ads. See Google's own policy: Google Privacy Policy.
  • App Store Billing The optional "Remove Ads" purchase (£2.99) is handled entirely through Apple's App Store / Google Play Billing. We never collect or store your payment details.
  • Native Share Sheet Sharing a snapshot of your garden briefly writes an image file to your device's own temporary app cache so the share sheet can attach it, then uses your device's native share sheet. What you choose to share and with whom is entirely up to you - we don't see or record it, and the temporary file isn't uploaded anywhere by us.

3. How We Use Collected Data

Automatically gathered device data is used exclusively to serve and measure banner/interstitial ads, and is never combined with any personal profile we hold, since we hold none.

4. Your Rights & Choices (Opt-Out)

  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to manage ad tracking permissions.
  • Android: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads to opt out of ad personalization.

5. Children's Privacy

Little Allotment does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.
