Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?
Tap the ribbon icon in the top bar, then "Restore Purchases". As long as you're signed in with the same Apple/Google ID used for the purchase, your ad-free status restores instantly for free.
Why can I only tend my garden once a day?
Everyone gets the same token pool and zone layout each day, and one allocation each - that's what makes it a genuine daily challenge you can compare with friends. Want to solve more allocation puzzles? Switch to Practice for a spare round any time - it won't touch your real garden or streak.
What happens if I miss a day?
Your garden still grows a little on its own while you're away (capped at a few days' worth), but it's far less than an actively tended day. Your streak resets though - it only continues if you tend on consecutive days, so the next day you play starts a fresh streak of 1.
Why isn't dumping all my tokens into the best zone the right move?
Every zone gives diminishing returns the more tokens you put into it - so the highest-scoring split is almost always spread across two or more zones rather than one. Your score each day is shown as a percentage of the best possible allocation.
Does sharing my garden give away today's puzzle?
No - the shared image just shows your garden's current growth and your day's score, not the token pool or zone details anyone else would need to solve their own puzzle.
