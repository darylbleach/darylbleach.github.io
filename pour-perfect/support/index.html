Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase?
Tap the 💎 button, then "Restore Purchases". As long as you're signed in with the same Apple/Google ID used for the purchase, your ad-free status restores instantly for free.
I'm stuck on a level - what can I do?
Use the 💡 hint button to highlight a valid next move, or ↩️ undo your last pour and try a different approach. Every level Pour Perfect generates is verified solvable before it's shown to you.
Can I sync my progress across devices?
Not currently - your furthest level is saved locally on your device. Cross-device sync may come in a future update.
