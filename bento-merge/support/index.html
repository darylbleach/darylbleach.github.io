Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my bento box overflow?
If merged pieces stack above the hazard line for more than a few seconds, the box overflows and the run ends. Try to merge pieces near the top of the stack quickly to bring the pile back down.
How do I restore "Remove Ads"?
Open the in-game Menu and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you're signed in with the same Apple/Google ID used originally, it will restore instantly for free.
Can I sync my best score across devices?
Currently, your best score is saved locally to your device only. Cloud sync via Game Center/Google Play Games is being considered for a future update.
