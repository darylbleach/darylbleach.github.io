Welcome to the FPL Assistant support hub. If a prediction looks off, a purchase hasn't unlocked, or your league standings won't load, we're here to help.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to log in with my FPL account?
No. FPL Assistant never asks for your official Fantasy Premier League login. To view Mini-League standings, simply enter the public League ID found in your league's settings on the official FPL site or app.
Why didn't my league standings load?
The Leagues tab labels the standings view using the ID you enter, alongside sample rival data for the banter feature - it doesn't pull live standings from the official FPL servers. If the card isn't appearing, make sure you've entered a League ID and tapped Import.
How do I restore "Remove Ads" or the Team Optimizer?
Open the Store tab and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you're signed in with the same Apple/Google ID used originally, your purchases will restore for free.
How accurate are the price change predictions?
Price swing predictions are estimates based on publicly available ownership and transfer trend data, intended as a helpful guide rather than a guarantee - official price changes are decided solely by the Fantasy Premier League game.
