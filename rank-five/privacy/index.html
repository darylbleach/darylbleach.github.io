Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected, used, and secured by Rank Five ("the Game") when played on mobile iOS or Android devices.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

Rank Five is a free-to-play puzzle game supported by third-party advertising and one optional in-app purchase. To facilitate these services, we use standard industry software development kits (SDKs) which may collect certain device-level information:

  • Technical Data: Your device model, operating system version, and system language, used to serve contextual (non-personalised) advertising. The Game does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Game Data: Your streak, daily results, and ad-free purchase state are stored locally on your own device using local storage - never on a server we control.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google AdMob We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial ads. The Game does not request tracking permission, so adverts are contextual rather than personalised. You can change or revoke your ad consent choice any time from the in-app Shop ("Manage Ad Consent"). See Google's own policy: Google Privacy Policy.
  • App Store Billing The optional "Remove Ads" purchase (£2.99) is handled entirely through Apple's App Store / Google Play Billing. We never collect or store your payment details.
  • Native Share Sheet Sharing your daily result uses your device's own share sheet. What you choose to share and with whom is entirely up to you - we don't see or record it.

3. How We Use Collected Data

Automatically gathered device data is used exclusively to serve and measure banner/interstitial ads, and is never combined with any personal profile we hold, since we hold none.

4. Your Rights & Choices (Opt-Out)

  • In-app: Open the Shop and tap "Manage Ad Consent" to revisit the Google consent form if you're in a region where it applies.
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking for system-level tracking controls.
  • Android: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads to opt out of ad personalization.

5. Children's Privacy

Rank Five does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.
