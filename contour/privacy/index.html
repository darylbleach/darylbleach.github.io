Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected, used, and secured by Contour ("the Game") when played on mobile iOS or Android devices.

1. Information We Collect Automatically

Contour is a free-to-play puzzle game supported by third-party advertising and one optional in-app purchase. To facilitate these services, we use standard industry software development kits (SDKs) which may collect certain device-level information:

  • Device Identifiers: Mobile advertising identifiers (such as Apple's IDFA) used to deliver ads.
  • Technical Data: Your device model, operating system version, and general region.
  • Game Data: Your current level, Ink balance, and ad-free purchase state are stored locally on your own device using local storage - never on a server we control. Every level's shape is generated on your device from its level number; nothing about the puzzles themselves is ever transmitted anywhere.

2. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google AdMob We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial ads. See Google's own policy: Google Privacy Policy.
  • App Store Billing The optional "Remove Ads" purchase (£2.99) is handled entirely through Apple's App Store / Google Play Billing. We never collect or store your payment details.

3. How We Use Collected Data

Automatically gathered device data is used exclusively to serve and measure banner/interstitial ads, and is never combined with any personal profile we hold, since we hold none.

4. Your Rights & Choices (Opt-Out)

  • In-app: Open the Shop and tap "Manage Ad Consent" at any time to review or change your ad personalisation choice.
  • iOS: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to manage ad tracking permissions.
  • Android: Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads to opt out of ad personalisation.

5. Children's Privacy

Contour does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children.
