Frequently Asked Questions

Why won't the board let me place a shape?
Each rectangle must contain exactly one numbered clue, matching its area precisely (width × height). If a shape overlaps another or doesn't cover a clue cell correctly, it will be rejected - try resizing or repositioning it.
How do I restore my purchases?
If you have reinstalled the game or moved to a new device, open Settings within the app and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you are signed in with the same Apple/Google ID used originally, your Remove Ads and Infinite Hints status will be restored for free.
I bought a hint pack but my balance hasn't updated.
Please make sure your device has an active internet connection and restart the app. If the hints still haven't appeared after a few minutes, contact us below with your purchase receipt and we'll credit your account promptly.
Can I change the colour palette?
The board colours are fixed as part of the Grid Zen look - there's no separate Customise menu or unlockable palettes. Valid rectangles tint to match their clue colour so you can tell groups apart while you play.
