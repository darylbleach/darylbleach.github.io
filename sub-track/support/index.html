Frequently Asked Questions

Does SubTrackr connect to my bank account?
No. SubTrackr never connects to any bank, card, or account. You enter each subscription's details yourself, and everything is calculated from what you've entered - nothing is verified against real transactions.
Can SubTrackr actually cancel my subscriptions for me?
No app can safely do that without holding your login details for every service you subscribe to, which SubTrackr deliberately never asks for. What it does do: save a direct link to wherever you go to cancel each subscription, plus a shortcut to Apple's own subscription manager for anything billed through the App Store - so you're not hunting for the right page when you decide to cancel.
Why doesn't my monthly total exactly match my bank statement?
Yearly and weekly subscriptions are converted to a monthly-equivalent figure (a yearly price divided across 12 months, for example) so everything can be compared on one dashboard. It's an estimate for comparison, not a live read of what's actually been charged.
How do renewal reminders work?
A local notification is scheduled a few days before each active subscription's renewal date. If a subscription's next renewal is sooner than that, you'll still get a reminder, just with less notice. Reminders are rescheduled automatically whenever you add, edit, cancel, or delete a subscription.
How do I restore my Premium purchase?
Open the Premium modal from the footer and tap "Restore Purchases". As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, it restores instantly at no extra cost.
What's the free tier limit?
Free accounts can track 5 active subscriptions at once. Unlocking Premium (a one-time £2.99 purchase) removes that limit entirely and adds a spend-by-category breakdown, plus removes the banner ad.
